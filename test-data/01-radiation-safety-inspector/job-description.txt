The officer will conduct inspection and licensing activities, collect information required for licence applications, prepare programmes for regulatory inspections, and conduct follow-up inspections to verify remedial actions. The officer will perform radiation surveillance and quality-control tests on radiation premises and equipment, prepare inspection reports, maintain a register of violations, coordinate compliance monitoring with zonal personnel, and support QC and QA programmes for diagnostic radiography, medical imaging and therapy. The applicant should hold a Bachelor Degree in Physics, Nuclear Physics or an equivalent field.
